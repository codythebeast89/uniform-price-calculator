Privacy Policy

QMC Uniform Price Calculator · Last updated: August 29, 2026

This policy describes how the USAR Quartermaster Corps Uniform Price Calculator (“the App”) handles information when you sign in with Roblox.

Who we are

The App is a community tool for USAR Quartermaster Corps members to estimate uniform order prices. It is not an official Roblox or U.S. Army product.

Information we collect

When you log in with Roblox OAuth, we may receive or look up:

  • Roblox user ID, username, and display name
  • Avatar thumbnail URL
  • Roblox group memberships (to confirm USAR membership and rank/unit hints)
  • Account age / birthdate signals Roblox provides (only to enforce age and account-age rules)
  • Awards associated with your Roblox username from the QMC awards database

Guests can use the calculator without logging in; no Roblox account data is collected in that case.

How we use it

  • Authenticate you and enforce login rules (USAR membership, minimum account age, age 13+)
  • Pre-fill calculator fields (username, paygrade/rank, unit path hints)
  • Limit award selections to awards on record for your username
  • Maintain a signed-in session (browser token / cookie, up to 7 days) so you stay logged in

Storage and retention

Session tokens are stored in your browser (local storage and/or a cookie) and expire after up to 7 days, or when you log out. We do not sell personal data. Server logs may briefly record technical request data for security and debugging.

Third parties

Login uses Roblox’s OAuth service. Awards data comes from the QMC awards spreadsheet maintained by Quartermaster Corps staff. Hosting may use GitHub Pages (static UI) and infrastructure operated by the App maintainers (API).

Children

The App is intended for users who meet Roblox’s own age requirements. As an additional signal, sign-in requires the Roblox account to be at least 30 days old and a member of the USAR Roblox group. When Roblox shares a verified birthdate with us, we also enforce a 13+ check directly — but Roblox does not always provide this, so in practice age assurance mainly relies on Roblox’s own account requirements plus our account-age and group-membership checks.

Your choices

You can log out in the App (clears the local session token). You can revoke the App’s access in your Roblox account settings. You may also contact the maintainers via USAR / QMC community channels to ask questions about this policy.

Changes

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date above will change when we do.
